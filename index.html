3D Object Scanner

Turn a real object into an interactive 3D model using nothing but your camera.

1
Capture. Take 16–24 photos circling the object (or slowly rotate the object in front of a fixed camera).
2
Reconstruct. The app carves a 3D shape from the outlines in your photos and colors it using the actual pixels you captured.
3
View & export. Rotate it in 3D, then download a real .obj/.ply model file.
For best results
  • Use a plain, contrasting background (a table or the floor works)
  • Keep the object centered and about the same distance in every shot
  • Space your photos evenly around a full 360° turn
  • Even, soft lighting — avoid strong shadows

Step 1 — Capture the empty background

Photograph the empty surface before placing your object on it. This makes separating the object from the background far more accurate. Optional, but recommended.

Step 2 — Capture the object

Take at least 8 photos, spaced evenly around the object.
